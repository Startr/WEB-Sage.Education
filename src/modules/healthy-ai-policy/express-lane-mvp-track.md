## Express Lane

**Purpose:** The express lane through this module. A leadership team with 3 hours and 30 minutes of pre-work leaves with a shippable policy skeleton — vision, defaults, owners, dates — and an explicit list of what the review cycle will finish. For the full facilitated experience, see `facilitation_guide.md`.

**Assumption:** the team did the pre-work. Without it, add 25 minutes (cold-start briefing: starter-kit walkthrough + hopes/risks round).

---

## Pre-work (30 min at home, per member)

1. Read `ai_policy_starter_kit.md` (15 min).
2. Note your top 3 hopes and top 3 risks about AI in your school (10 min).
3. List every AI tool already in use in your building, by staff or students — including ones leadership may not know about (5 min; feeds the starting tool list).

## Definition of done (the MVP contract)

You leave with a **shippable skeleton**, not a finished policy:
- Axis-naming record + vision draft
- All 6 core sections + 4 appendices touched — every bracket filled or explicitly marked DEFERRED
- Default level set (AI-Optional + disclosure, unless you chose otherwise)
- Disclosure statement adopted (your school's version of the when/why/how)
- Step 0 scheduled with date + owner
- Review date + owner locked; contact-sheet owners named; 3 monitoring signals picked
- Photos of every artefact in one shared folder

**Explicitly deferred to the review cycle** — written down, not silently dropped (each maps to its full-module home):
- Formal citation styles → Part 3, §3.5
- Full tool-approval panel sittings → Part 2, §2.5 (the starting list adopted today stands until then)
- Board adoption paperwork → Part 2, board box
- Remaining 5 scenarios → Part 3, §3.6
- IEP-team AI-accommodation decisions → Part 2, §2.4
- Translation check → Activity 1-A; emotional audit → Step 0

## 30-day check-in (doubles as pilot feedback)

**Right after the session (facilitator, 10 min):** per block, log planned vs. actual minutes; note confusion points (what did the team misunderstand?) and asks (what did they request that wasn't here?); attach artefact photos. Confidence vote: *could we defend this policy tomorrow?* (fist-of-five).

**Day 30 (owner, 20 min):** did staff accept the defaults? Did parents understand the letter? What broke first? What belongs in the off-cycle review, if any?

**Pilot schools:** send the above plus one paragraph — *what would you change about this track?* — back to the module team. That's the data that turns this MVP into version two.
